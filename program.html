The 11th International Workshop on Crowd-Based Requirements Engineering (CrowdRE'27)



23–24 August 2027
In conjunction with RE 2027
Milan, Italy

Program

The detailed schedule will be published after paper notifications (28 June 2027) and the workshop program deadline (10 July 2027). Exact day (23 and/or 24 August) will be assigned by the RE'27 organizers.

CrowdRE'27 is planned as a workshop collocated with the RE 2027 conference at Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy. RE'27 workshops take place on Monday 23 and/or Tuesday 24 August 2027. The workshop intends to promote lively discussions among participants from both industry and academia.

Workshop Format

CrowdRE'27 is intended as a one-day workshop. The program typically features:

  • Keynote or invited talk — TBA
  • Paper sessions — Presentations followed by discussant-led discussion
  • Interactive / working session — Joint discussion aligned with the workshop theme
  • Closing — Wrap-up by the organizers
