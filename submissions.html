The 11th International Workshop on Crowd-Based Requirements Engineering (CrowdRE'27)



23–24 August 2027
In conjunction with RE 2027
Milan, Italy

Submissions

Submission instructions will be finalized with the CrowdRE'27 Call for Papers (due 8 March 2027 per the RE'27 Workshops track). EasyChair and page limits below are provisional placeholders based on recent CrowdRE editions.

Submission site: TBA (EasyChair)

Submission deadline: 31 May 2027 AoE time (provisional)

We typically welcome original submissions from research and practice in the following categories:

  • Technical solution papers providing research results with an early validation, which may also include replication studies and tool showcases (4–6 pages + 1 page for references).
  • Experience reports providing insights on existing RE practice and potential for application in settings that involve a crowd (4–6 pages + 1 page for references).
  • Problem statements explaining industry problems in settings with a large group of stakeholders (2–3 pages).
  • Vision statements explaining exploratory ideas, especially toward technology transfer into practice (2–3 pages).

Submissions must describe original works that have not been previously published, are not currently submitted elsewhere, and address at least one of the workshop topics.

Submissions must be written in English and formatted according to the RE 2027 Formatting Instructions (IEEE conference template). Accepted papers are expected to appear in the joint RE workshop proceedings in the IEEE Digital Library, subject to RE'27 policies.

At least one author of every accepted manuscript is expected to attend the workshop and present their research. All workshop participants must register through the RE'27 registration system.

Important Dates
  • Abstracts (optional): 24 May 2027
  • Paper Submission: 31 May 2027
  • Notification: 28 June 2027
  • Camera Ready: 12 July 2027
  • Workshop: 23–24 August 2027

All deadlines at 23:59:59 AoE. Provisional until the CrowdRE'27 CfP is published.

Workshop Topics

Detailed topics of interest will be published with the Call for Papers. CrowdRE typically welcomes submissions on:

  • Crowd-based elicitation, analysis, prioritization, validation, and evolution of requirements
  • User feedback mining, crowd as stakeholder, and large-scale stakeholder involvement
  • Applications of AI / GenAI, machine learning, and natural language processing for CrowdRE
  • Human values, ethics, diversity & inclusion, explainability, privacy, and sustainability in CrowdRE
  • Human–AI collaboration and new forms of teaming in crowd-based RE (aligned with the RE'27 theme)
  • Case studies, industry practice, and interdisciplinary approaches involving CrowdRE
