The 11th International Workshop on Crowd-Based Requirements Engineering (CrowdRE'27)



23–24 August 2027
In conjunction with RE 2027
Milan, Italy

Overview

Content for CrowdRE'27 is under preparation. Exact workshop day, theme details, call for papers, and submission link will be published after workshop acceptance and the official website deadline (22 February 2027). Dates below follow the RE'27 Workshops track.

CrowdRE has helped requirements engineering (RE) scale up to settings with thousands to millions of users of a (software) product. Online media enable stakeholders to form a large and heterogeneous group that can be denoted as a 'crowd'. The Crowd-Based Requirements Engineering paradigm integrates appropriate methods and tools for dealing with crowds in a single framework.

Since 2015, the International Workshop on Crowd-Based Requirements Engineering (CrowdRE) has been facilitating interactive discussions between scientists and representatives of industry through keynotes, interactive sessions, and paper submissions presenting novel and innovative ideas on involving the crowd and collecting, harmonizing, analyzing, and interpreting user feedback.

Alignment with RE'27

The Human Element in RE: Evolving Skills and New Forms of Teaming

The main theme of RE'27 examines how RE work is evolving and how people collaborate with one another and with AI systems. CrowdRE'27 will continue to explore the role of crowds, user feedback, and socio-technical collaboration in requirements engineering. Specific workshop themes and topics of interest will be announced with the Call for Papers.
